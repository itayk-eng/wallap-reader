# WALLAP Report Reader

Open a WALLAP results report (PDF) in the browser and get:

- a summary of the final wall section with anchor levels and maximum anchor forces
- bending moment, shear and displacement envelopes drawn on the section
- loads, stage-by-stage results and anchor forces
- an anchor design helper (spacing and strand count against the consultant's force per metre)
- drawings at 1:100 as DXF (for CAD), PDF, or straight to the printer

Everything runs in the browser. Reports you open are read on your own computer and are not uploaded anywhere.
The values shown are read from the WALLAP report; nothing is re-analysed.

Live site: https://itayk-eng.github.io/wallap-reader/
